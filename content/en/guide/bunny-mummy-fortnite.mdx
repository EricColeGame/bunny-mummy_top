## How Fortnite Cosmetic Availability Actually Works

Understanding the system saves you from refreshing the shop for months on end. Most Fortnite cosmetics fall into three buckets: Battle Pass rewards, Item Shop rotations, and event-locked bundles. Each behaves differently, and mixing them up is the number one reason players get frustrated.

Battle Pass items are tied to a specific season and generally never return once that season ends. Item Shop cosmetics rotate in and out on a daily schedule — the shop refreshes at 00:00 UTC, which lands in the late afternoon or early evening in the United States depending on daylight saving time. Event-locked items appear during limited-time events and typically leave when the event wraps up.

| Source | Can It Return? | Best Way to Track It |
|---|---|---|
| Battle Pass reward | Generally no | Finish the pass before it expires |
| Item Shop rotation | Yes, on Epic's schedule | Check the daily shop reset |
| Event bundle | Sometimes, at later events | Watch official event announcements |
| Special promotion | Rarely | Follow official channels closely |

| Seasonal Window | Why It Matters | What to Do |
|---|---|---|
| Fall / Halloween events | Spooky themes dominate rotations | Check the shop every single day |
| Spring / Easter events | Rabbit themes get their moment | Watch bundle tabs, not just singles |
| Winter events | Mixed themes sometimes appear | Budget V-Bucks in advance |
| Summer events | Rotations widen overall | Great time to grab missing set pieces |

For official timing and event announcements, keep an eye on [Fortnite's official website](https://www.fortnite.com/), which posts shop and event news directly from Epic Games.

**Is the bunny mummy Fortnite skin a limited-time item?**
It is a rotating cosmetic rather than a permanent store item, which means it appears in the Item Shop during specific windows and then leaves. Rotating items can return, unlike Battle Pass rewards, so patience usually pays off.

There is no fixed schedule. Epic decides rotations based on player demand and upcoming events, and community reports suggest hybrid holiday skins tend to resurface around seasonal celebrations.

No. Battle Pass rewards are tied to a specific season and are generally not sold separately. If a skin appears in the Item Shop, it is a direct purchase rather than a pass unlock.

If you want the matching back bling and harvesting tool, the bundle is almost always the better value. If you only care about the character model, a single-item purchase keeps your V-Bucks in reserve for the next rotation.
