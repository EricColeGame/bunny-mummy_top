## Gameplay Tips for Wearing the Bunny Mummy

Cosmetics do not change hitboxes or stats in Fortnite, but they absolutely change how you are perceived. Here is how to think about that.

| Factor | Reality | Practical Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Hitbox | Identical to other outfits | No advantage or disadvantage |
| Visibility | Bright accents can stand out | Play tighter angles in open fields |
| Silhouette | Ears create a distinct outline | Expect to be recognized at range |
| Mind games | Unusual skins read as confident | Use it to bait aggressive pushes |
| Team readability | Easy for squadmates to spot you | Helpful in chaotic endgames |

The ears are the interesting part. A taller, more distinctive outline means opponents can identify you faster, but it also means your own squad can track you in a messy build fight. Some players lean into this and run the Bunny Mummy as a deliberate attention magnet, drawing fire while teammates rotate.

If you prefer a stealthier approach, pair the outfit with darker back bling and wraps to reduce the number of bright focal points on your character.
