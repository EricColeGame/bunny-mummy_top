## How V-Bucks Actually Work

V-Bucks are Fortnite's premium currency. You can buy them with real money, earn a limited amount through gameplay, or receive them through certain passes and promotions. They are tied to your Epic account, not to a single platform, so a balance earned on one device generally follows you to another when accounts are linked properly.

The important nuance is that V-Bucks are a middle layer. You spend real money on a V-Bucks pack, then spend V-Bucks on cosmetics. That extra step is why players often lose track of what a skin truly costs.
