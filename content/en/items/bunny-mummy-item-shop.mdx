## Common Mistakes With the Bunny Mummy Item Shop

Most bad purchases follow a predictable script. Avoiding these five habits will save you more currency than any discount ever will.

- **Buying in the final hour.** Urgency compresses judgment. Decide during the calm middle of the window.
- **Trusting chat over the interface.** If a claim about the bunny mummy item shop is not visible in the shop itself or in official notes, treat it as rumor.
- **Ignoring the currency source.** Spending premium currency on a seasonal cosmetic means skipping something else later.
- **Assuming a rerun.** Some items return; many do not. If you would genuinely regret missing it, that is your answer — but only if you would use it.
- **Buying for resale without checking tradability.** Account-bound items have zero resale value, no matter how good they look.

There is also a quieter mistake: buying because the item is popular rather than because you like it. Cosmetics you never equip are the most expensive items in any shop.

## FAQ

**Can I trade or resell the bunny mummy item?**
